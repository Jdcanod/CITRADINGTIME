About us

C.I Trading Time S.A.S.

We are a Colombian international trading company dedicated to B2B foreign trade: we connect Colombian companies and producers with buyers in global markets, turning those connections into sustainable, long-term business relationships.

Under the management of Álvaro de Jesús Echeverri Castrillón, we work to bring the best of Colombia to the world, connecting products with purpose and history to companies committed to sustainability.

We make our purpose a reality together with partner companies in the United States and Panama — Raso Maker LLC and Interlabs — which allow us to take our producers' differentiated products directly to the end buyer, under an integration project for international trade.

"We connect products with purpose and history to companies committed to sustainability."
Mission

To position differentiated products from Colombian micro, small and medium-sized enterprises in a sustainable way, through a direct distribution model that integrates the producer with the specialized point of sale; from the manufacturing of the product to its placement in the direct buyer's space, under fair trade conditions. Likewise, connecting the buyer with the history and purpose of the product and the enterprise behind it, and guaranteeing an efficient International Physical Distribution (IPD) chain for everyone involved.

Vision

By 2027, to have fifty differentiated, high-value-added products from at least ten Colombian small and medium-sized enterprises positioned in international markets, under a direct distribution and fair trade model. Likewise, to increase the sales and profitability of Colombian enterprises by more than ten percent per year, once the product is positioned in each market.
